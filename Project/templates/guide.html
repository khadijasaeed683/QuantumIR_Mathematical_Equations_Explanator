Understanding Quantum IR

QIR

What is Quantum IR?

Quantum Information Retrieval (QIR) is an emerging interdisciplinary field that applies quantum computing principles to enhance traditional information retrieval systems, enabling:

  • Exponential speedup in search algorithms through quantum parallelism
  • Enhanced relevance scoring using quantum superposition states
  • Quantum-inspired ranking algorithms for improved search results
  • Pattern recognition leveraging quantum entanglement
  • Optimized query processing with quantum circuit design principles

Yes! Our system includes Optical Character Recognition (OCR) capabilities that automatically process scanned documents. While text-based PDFs yield optimal results, scanned documents are processed with advanced OCR to extract readable text before analysis.

Absolutely. Your PDFs are processed in a secure environment and are not stored permanently. Files are automatically deleted after analysis completion, and no personal or research data is retained. All processing occurs in memory without permanent storage.
